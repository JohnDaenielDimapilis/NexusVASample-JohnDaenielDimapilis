# Proposed conversation and schedule review terms

**Draft for organizational, Data Protection Officer, and legal review. Not an executed agreement.** The existing prototype contained no signed client or VA privacy agreement to amend. This text can be incorporated into the applicable service agreement after approval.

1. Client and VA request conversations and calendars are available to the participants according to their assigned work and permissions. Operational Admin status alone does not grant access to private messages, attachments, availability, or live appointments.
2. A documented complaint, dispute, security issue, or other defined investigation may be handled through a case. The case identifies its purpose, reason, requester, assigned reviewer, independently approved scope, and access expiry. A Support Reviewer or Privacy Officer receives read-only access only to the approved material and period. Attachment access requires separate approval.
3. Schedule History review is limited to eligible historical appointment outcomes and previous slots. It does not show future appointments or the new future slot of a rescheduled appointment.
4. Emergency access must be justified, time limited, logged, and independently reviewed after use. Misuse may lead to removal of access and organizational action under applicable policy.
5. The organization will publish its verified privacy contact, lawful bases, retention schedule, rights process, cross-border processing information, and incident process in the final notice. No retention period is set by this prototype.

**Open approval:** Determine whether these terms require client and VA acknowledgment, which organization is the personal information controller, and how existing agreements are updated and recorded.
