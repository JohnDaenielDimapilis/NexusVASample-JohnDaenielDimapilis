Privacy and review notice

Draft for organizational review. This local test version demonstrates proposed controls. The designated Data Protection Officer or qualified counsel must approve this notice, the lawful basis, retention rules, and contact details before production use.

Private conversations

Client and VA messages belong to the participants in an assigned request. Admins do not receive routine message access. A documented dispute, report, safety concern, or investigation can lead to a Conversation Review case. The case records a purpose, reason, requester, assigned reviewer, approved time range, independent approval, status, and expiry. A reviewer can read only the approved range while authorization is active. Attachment access requires separate justification and a short-lived link. Reviewers cannot send or change messages or read receipts through the review tool.

Calendars and schedule history

Clients and VAs use their own authorized calendars and shared appointments. Admins do not receive live or upcoming client or VA calendars. Schedule History allows an authorized case reviewer to see eligible past appointments, past cancellations, past no-shows, and previous slots from rescheduled appointments. A new future slot is not included. A cancellation made before the original appointment date remains hidden until that original period has passed.

Accountability

Administrative access decisions, reviews, approved and denied requests, attachment access, permission changes, and emergency use are recorded in a protected audit trail. Logs record actor, time, case, purpose or justification, scope, and outcome. They are not intended to include message contents. Emergency access is short lived and requires later independent review.

Your questions and rights

Ask the organization's designated privacy contact about access, correction, or a privacy concern through its established support channel. The organization must add verified contact details and its approved retention, lawful-basis, and rights-handling information before publishing this notice.

Local demo limitation

Browser demo accounts and records are stored in this browser for prototype testing. They do not provide server-side privacy protection. Use sample data only. Connected mode requires the database migrations and attachment-signing function described in the project documentation.
